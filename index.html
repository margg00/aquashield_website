How it works

Four steps from pipe to alert.

Install

Clamp-on sensors go onto existing pipes in under 15 minutes each. No pipe cutting.

Learn

A model per measuring point learns your building's normal flow in 10-minute steps.

Detect

Deviations from normal are flagged and ranked by severity.

Localise

A digital twin of the plumbing narrows the leak to a riser or a building section.
