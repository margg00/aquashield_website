## Product (nav: Product, H1: How AquaShield works)

**Title:** How AquaShield works: sensors, AI, digital twin (47 chars)
**Meta description:** Clamp-on ultrasonic and pressure sensors, a model per measuring point that learns normal flow, and a digital twin that narrows a leak to a riser. (146 chars)

### Direct answer (52 words)

H1: How AquaShield works
AquaShield detects and localises leaks in complex pipe networks using non-invasive sensors and AI. Clamp-on sensors go onto existing pipes. A model per measuring point learns the building's normal flow. A digital twin of the plumbing narrows a deviation to a riser or a building section. Alerts are reviewed with your maintenance team.

### Objection 1: What does an install look like? (78 words)

H2: What does an install look like?
Ultrasonic flow sensors clamp onto the outside of existing pipes. Pressure sensors go on existing taps. Each sensor takes under 15 minutes. No pipe cutting, no shutdown of the water supply. Every rollout starts with a walkthrough with your maintenance team, on site, remote or from plumbing diagrams. Placement is built on the failure modes they already know. There is no one-size-fits-all layout. After the first building, maintenance teams can self-install.
Visual: Sensor diagram, clamp-on ultrasonic on a riser, pressure sensor on a tap. [TO CONFIRM: install photo, Tim]

### Objection 2: What data do I get? (84 words)

H2: What data do I get?
A dashboard with every measuring point, its live flow against its learned baseline, and the alert list ranked by severity. Each alert names the building, the riser or section, how far flow is from normal and for how long, and what to check first. Data coverage per site is shown so you know when a sensor is offline. Alerts go to the people you name, by email [TO CONFIRM: alert channels, Marguerite]. Nothing is sent before it has been reviewed.
Visual: Sample dataset view, one measuring point over seven days, baseline band and deviation.

### Objection 3: How precise is the localisation? (71 words)

H2: How precise is the localisation?
Honest answer: to the riser or the building section, not to the pipe segment. At one site the system narrowed a deviation to a single riser. At another it pointed to one side of the building. [TO CONFIRM: wording, Marguerite] Precision grows with the number of measuring points and with every incident the model learns from. We state no accuracy percentage until it is measured across enough sites.
Visual: Digital twin, risers highlighted, one red.

### The machine learning (68 words)

H2: The machine learning
Each measuring point gets its own model. It learns that building's normal flow in 10-minute steps: night baseline, morning peak, weekday against weekend. A deviation is a flow pattern that normal does not explain. The digital twin maps which sensors see the same water, so a deviation can be placed in the network. False alarms your team flags go back into the model. Specialised machine learning for hydraulic systems, not a generic anomaly detector.

### Sensors (44 words)

H2: The sensors
Clamp-on ultrasonic flow sensors on risers and supply lines. Pressure sensors on existing taps. Wired or battery [TO CONFIRM: battery sensor availability, Marguerite]. Readings every 10 minutes. Sensors report data coverage so the dashboard shows when one is offline.

### The underwriter story (84 words)

H2: Severity is driven by time.
A hidden leak runs until someone notices. Every extra day adds secondary damage: subfloors, drywall, neighbouring units, business interruption. Earlier detection shortens the loss duration, and that is the lever on severity. Live flow data per riser adds a second value: a risk signal that does not exist today, from buildings that are owner-operated and already instrumented. No accuracy figures yet. 1. Insurer-subsidised hardware. The carrier funds sensors in the insured portfolio.

4. Risk data. Live building data feeds risk scoring and renewal.
5. Claims response. An alert triggers the claims process before the tenant calls.
Which one fits depends on your book. The 30-minute call is where we find out.
