Severity is driven by time.

A hidden leak runs until someone notices. Every extra day adds secondary damage: subfloors, drywall, neighbouring units, business interruption. Earlier detection shortens the loss duration, and that is the lever on severity. Live flow data per riser adds a second value: a risk signal that underwriting does not have today, from buildings that are owner-operated and already instrumented.

Five ways to work together

  1. Insurer-subsidised hardware

    The carrier funds sensors in the insured portfolio.

  2. Premium discount

    Monitored buildings earn a lower rate.

  3. Bundled rider

    Monitoring sold with the policy.

  4. Risk data

    Live building data feeds risk scoring and renewal.

  5. Claims response

    An alert triggers the claims process before the tenant calls.

Which one fits depends on your book. The 30-minute call is where we find out.

What we share, and what stays with the owner

We can: aggregated flow deviations per building, alert counts and durations, data coverage, with the owner's consent.
Stays with the owner: unit-level consumption, tenant data, anything the owner has not released. The owner stays in control of their data.
