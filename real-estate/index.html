Leaks are discovered, not detected.

A tenant calls. A ceiling stains. Water shows up on a basement floor. By then the leak has been running for days or weeks. Finding it can take up to five site visits and a wall opened each time. Occupancy, energy and HVAC all have a live view. Water does not. AquaShield gives your maintenance team that view: every riser watched, deviations ranked by severity, and a location to check first.

Questions from portfolio teams

We have smart water meters. Is this the same?

No. Meters count consumption per unit. AquaShield watches flow at the riser and learns what normal looks like, so it sees a leak that no single meter would.

Do we need to open walls?

No. Sensors clamp onto accessible pipe in risers, basements and shafts.

What does our maintenance team have to do?

Clamp on the sensors, or let us do it. Then act on the alerts: each one names the riser and the floors to check first.

Does it work in old buildings?

Yes. The sensors clamp onto any accessible pipe, old or new.

How many buildings to start?

One. Roll out when you have seen it work.

Can our insurer see the data?

Only if you decide so. Some portfolios share it to negotiate terms. See the insurance page.
