## Why one building first

Portfolio rollouts fail when they start with a portfolio-wide plan. Procurement, legal and IT all get involved before anyone has seen an alert. The faster route is one building, chosen for proof, with everything else decided after the first alert has landed on a maintenance manager's screen.

## Step 1: Map

Send the plumbing plan, or walk us through the risers once. From the plan, AquaShield places the measuring points where they see the most water and builds the digital twin: which riser feeds which floors, which sensor sees which water. A building needs far fewer measuring points than it has flats. The first portfolio in Germany covers 22 buildings with 32.

## Step 2: Install

Under 15 minutes per sensor. The sensors clamp onto the outside of existing pipes and read through the wall, so there is no pipe cutting and no shutdown. A building is a day. Tenants do not notice.

## Step 3: Learn

Each measuring point gets its own model, and it spends the first weeks learning the building's normal flow: the night baseline, the morning peak, the weekly rhythm. No alerts yet. An alert before normal is known would be a guess.

## Step 4: Detect

Detection runs every 10 minutes. Deviations are ranked by severity and localised by the twin to the riser and the floors to check first. The maintenance team gets a place to go. Every alert is reviewed with the team and the outcome goes back into the model.

## Then roll out

After the first building, the portfolio's own team can clamp on the sensors. The plan for each next building is made the same way, the twin is generated, the models learn, and the dashboard grows building by building. At your pace.

## A rollout checklist

Before the first building:

- The plumbing plan, or one walk through the risers.
- Access to shafts and plant rooms on install day.
- The names of the people who should receive alerts.

After the first alert:

- The order of the next buildings, usually by water-damage history.
- Whether the portfolio's own team installs from building two, or AquaShield does.
- Whether the insurer should see aggregate data, which is the owner's decision.

Nothing on the list needs procurement to finish before the first building starts. That is the point of starting with one.
