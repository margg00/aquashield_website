How AquaShield works

AquaShield detects and localises leaks in complex pipe networks using non-invasive sensors and AI. Clamp-on sensors go onto existing pipes. Our model learns the building's normal flow. A digital twin of the plumbing narrows a deviation to a riser or a building section. Alerts are reviewed with your maintenance team.

What does an install look like?

Ultrasonic flow sensors clamp onto the outside of existing pipes. Under 15 minutes each, no pipe cutting, no shutdown of the water supply. From the sensor positions and the plumbing layout, AquaShield builds a digital twin of the building: which riser feeds which floors, which sensor sees which water. The model learns the building's normal flow from there. Your own team can clamp on the sensors.

Clamp-on ultrasonic flow sensor. Under 15 minutes each. No pipe cutting.

What data do I get?

A dashboard with every measuring point, its live flow against its learned baseline, and the alert list ranked by severity. Each alert names the building, the riser or section, how far flow is from normal and for how long, and what to check first. Data coverage per site is shown so you know when a sensor is offline. Alerts go to the people you name. Nothing is sent before it has been reviewed.

Sample chart: one measuring point over seven days, the learned baseline band and a deviation on the last two days.

How precise is the localisation?

To the riser or the building section. The digital twin narrows a deviation to the place to check first, so the search starts at the right riser and the right floors instead of behind the first wet wall. Precision grows with every measuring point and every incident the model learns from.

The digital twin: A model of the building's plumbing: risers, branches, which sensor sees which water. It is what turns a deviation into a place to check first.

The machine learning

Each measuring point gets its own model. It learns that building's normal flow in 10-minute steps: night baseline, morning peak, weekday against weekend. A deviation is a flow pattern that normal does not explain. The digital twin maps which sensors see the same water, so a deviation can be placed in the network. Your team's feedback goes straight back into the model. Specialised machine learning for hydraulic systems, not a generic anomaly detector.

Six measuring points, each with its learned baseline band and live flow. One riser departs from its baseline and is flagged.

The sensors

One sensor type, on existing pipework. Nothing is cut.

  • Clamp-on ultrasonic flow sensors on risers and supply lines
  • Under 15 minutes per sensor, no pipe cutting, no shutdown
  • A reading every 10 minutes from every measuring point
  • Coverage reported, so the dashboard shows when a sensor is offline

Questions we always get

Does the install interrupt the water supply?

No. Sensors clamp onto the outside of the pipe. The water stays on.

How long does a rollout take?

Fast, and it scales. Each sensor clamps on in under 15 minutes, with no pipe cutting and no shutdown, so a building is live in a day. Your own team can install the sensors, which means many buildings go live in parallel. The digital twin is built the same day and the model starts learning straight away.

Does it work with old pipes?

Yes. The sensors clamp onto any accessible pipe, old or new.

How do alerts stay relevant?

Alerts are ranked by severity and reviewed with your team before they escalate. Your feedback goes back into the model, so it gets sharper with every building.

Who sees the data?

You and the people you name. We do not sell data. See the privacy policy.

What does it cost?

Priced per building and measuring point, a fraction of one claim. We quote from your plumbing plan.
